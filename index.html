How it works

From customer context
to a coordinated next action.

Connect the signals, build relevant experiences and manage engagement across channels. Netcore’s official documentation covers segmentation, journeys, personalisation, analytics and messaging. [6]

01 / DATA

Know the relevant context.

Bring authorised attributes and behavioural signals into customer profiles and segments that support engagement.

02 / PERSONALISE

Make the experience relevant.

Use audience context to personalise web, app and messaging experiences with approved content and appropriate next steps.

03 / ORCHESTRATE

Coordinate the journey.

Connect triggers, audiences and follow-up messages, then use analytics to understand progress towards defined actions.

Trust & responsible delivery

Relevant engagement.
Within the right boundaries.

A proposed solution should reflect Prudential’s business rules and governance requirements, not bypass them.

Appropriate data & channel use

Use authorised data, eligible channels and customer preferences. Define communication limits and suppression conditions for each journey.

Content & launch accountability

Retain appropriate review of brand, language, financial promotions and customer communications—including AI-generated drafts.

Security & implementation design

Align access, hosting, retention, integration and security requirements with the technical design and the relevant product evidence.

Meaningful measurement

Agree success events and measurement design. Separate attributed results from demonstrated incremental impact.
